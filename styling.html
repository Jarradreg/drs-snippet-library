Styling guide.

Direct Retail Solutions PR

We use Discord’s official styling guide to make the quality of our messages the best that we can do. We do support:

  • Mentions, highlighted in blue. Type @name and Discord colours it for you — no formatting needed.
  • Pings, such as the public relations ping. These notify real people, so we use them sparingly and only when a reply is genuinely needed.
  • Emojis, even custom ones. Standard emoji and the server’s own custom emoji both render exactly as they do in Discord.
  • Text styling, like bold, italic, and underline. Surround a word with **, _ or __ and Discord does the rest.

Some features might not work

Strikethrough, for example, is something we haven’t really implemented in our system. We leave it out on purpose, because we want the library to be the easiest thing to run on everyone’s phones and computers without having too much lag.
